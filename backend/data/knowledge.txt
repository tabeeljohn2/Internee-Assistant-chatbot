Internee.pk is a virtual, AI-powered internship platform for Pakistani students.
Interns get tasks in the Task Portal. They click Guidelines to read the instructions, complete the work, upload project files to a shared cloud folder such as Google Drive, then click Submit Task and add the link for verification.
After successfully completing the assigned tasks and verification, Internee.pk issues a certificate. Certificates use the name on the account profile.
The Generative AI Internship has 5 tasks:
1) AI Personalized Learning Assistant
2) AI Resume Evaluator
3) Chatbot for Intern Queries (tools: Rasa, Dialogflow, OpenAI API, Flask)
4) AI Career Path Recommender (tools: Python, TensorFlow, OpenAI API)
5) Generative AI-Based Mock Interviewer (tools: Next.js, OpenAI API, Firebase).
The Internship dashboard in the portal shows the days remaining.
